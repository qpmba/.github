## Coverage
### Data Protection Officer
All businesses process and store data relating to identifiable living persons. This data must be subject to strict governance, robust security controls, and clearly defined accountability. Furthermore, organisations must be capable of demonstrating compliance through comprehensive evidence and documentation that can withstand detailed and intrusive regulatory, contractual, and operational audits. 

### Data Protection Rep
Compliance should not be a barrier to business growth. As your Data Protection Representative, we provide local expertise, pragmatic advice, simplified administration, and a highly responsive service. We act as a reliable partner, helping you meet regulatory obligations efficiently while maintaining confidence with regulators, customers, and auditors.

- Off-site self-paced e-learning courses
Training records and completion reporting

Whether delivered face-to-face or through self-paced online modules, our programmes help your teams understand their responsibilities and apply best practices with confidence.

### Risk Assessments
Understanding and managing risk is a fundamental component of effective data protection and information governance. Our risk assessment services help organisations identify, evaluate, and mitigate risks to personal data, ensuring that appropriate technical and organisational measures are in place.

- Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs)
- Privacy Impact Assessments (PIAs)
- Information Security Risk Assessments
- Third-Party and Supplier Risk Reviews
- International Data Transfer Risk Assessments
- Legitimate Interest Assessments (LIAs)
- Compliance Gap Analysis and Remediation Planning
- Audit Readiness Reviews
- Risk Registers and Ongoing Risk Monitoring
- Mitigation Planning and Control Recommendations
We provide practical, proportionate assessments that focus on real business risks, helping organisations demonstrate due diligence, strengthen compliance, and build confidence with customers, regulators, and stakeholders.
